1. Executive Summary

Bihar continues to rank among India’s lowest-performing states across multiple human development indicators. Despite improvements in literacy, maternal health, poverty reduction, and population outcomes over the past decade, the state faces persistent gaps in infrastructure, institutional capacity, and social equity.

This project conducts a deep-dive social indicators analysis across five critical domains — Education, Health, Crime & Safety, Social Inequality, and Demographics — using public datasets from NCRB, NFHS, MoSPI, NITI Aayog, Census, and the OGD Platform.

The objective is to quantify the scale of Bihar’s development challenges, understand underlying structural patterns, and present clear, actionable, data-backed recommendations for policymakers, NGOs, and analysts.

2. Project Objectives

This study aims to:

2.1 Analyze long-term trends

Identify how Bihar’s key social indicators have evolved from 2015–2024.

2.2 Benchmark Bihar against national standards

Compare Bihar’s performance to all-India averages and other low-performing states.

2.3 Uncover district-level disparities

Map inequalities across education, crime, poverty, and health outcomes.

2.4 Identify root causes

Explore structural constraints such as:

Resource distribution

Human capital gaps

Gender disparity

Rural–urban divide

Healthcare infrastructure limitations

2.5 Recommend targeted interventions

Deliver solutions that are realistic, implementable, and evidence-driven.
